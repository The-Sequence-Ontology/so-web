## Contact

The primary method of reaching members of the SO community is through SO's [GitHub Issue Tracker](https://github.com/The-Sequence-Ontology/SO-Ontologies/issues). If your question/comment/issue/etc. is particularly time sensitive, please email Evan Christensen ([evan.christensen@utah.edu](mailto:evan.christensen@utah.edu)).
